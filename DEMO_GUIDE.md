# Eventra 5–10 minute demonstration

## 0:00–1:00 — Platform and roles

Sign in as an administrator and show the existing event/registration platform. Explain that the original Phase 1–3 event workflows remain the system of record.

## 1:00–2:30 — Data Intelligence and honest data status

Open **Data Intelligence**. Show data quality, ML readiness, current-event outcome availability, source labels, temporal state, and prediction-point coverage. Expand Tech Summit and explain why its stale status and post-event timestamps disqualify it as a trustworthy ML outcome. Point out that legacy registrations are not attendance.

## 2:30–4:00 — Historical import workflow

Download the CSV template, select a prepared historical CSV with its source reference, preview valid/rejected rows, and confirm only after reviewing the reasons. Explain that accepted rows remain imported and unverified until the source document is reviewed.

## 4:00–5:30 — Synthetic model lab

Show the explicit **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA** label, fixed seed, generated dataset method, chronological split, baseline and Ridge metrics, feature importance, and holdout examples. State that the results do not represent production accuracy.

## 5:30–7:30 — Digital twin

Add two or three scenarios with different registrations, venue capacities, and expected check-in rates. Compare attendance, no-shows, utilization, risk, and the evidence-based recommendation. Emphasize **SIMULATION — NOT SAVED**.

## 7:30–9:00 — Organizer operations

Open organizer Event Intelligence. Show the deterministic readiness score and current planning estimate, then expand Data Quality and Prediction Point explanations. Clarify the difference between a rule-based analysis, historical outcome, forecast/planning estimate, synthetic experiment, and scenario.

## 9:00–10:00 — Close

Summarize: Eventra handles event operations and helps organizers understand both what the available evidence suggests and what it cannot yet support. Its ML readiness gate prevents the project from presenting demo data as real-world model performance.
